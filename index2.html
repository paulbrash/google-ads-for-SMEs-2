Why most small business Google Ads don't deliver

Google Ads is genuinely effective for small businesses — but only when it's set up correctly and managed consistently. Without that, it quietly drains budget while delivering little in return.

These are the issues we see in almost every account we look at.

01

Set up once, never touched

Without regular optimisation, performance degrades and budget leaks into searches that will never convert.

02

Targeting the wrong people

Broad keyword settings mean paying for clicks from people who were never going to become customers.

03

Ads that don't convert

Generic ad copy that doesn't match what searchers actually need drives clicks — but not enquiries.

04

Flying blind on performance

Without clear data on what's working, it's impossible to know which campaigns are driving real outcomes.

05

Following Google's advice

Google's own recommendations are designed to increase spend, not efficiency. Following them uncritically often makes things worse.

06

No time to learn it properly

Google Ads changes constantly. Running a business leaves little time to master it — and half-measures often do more harm than good.
